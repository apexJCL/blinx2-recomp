## Context

The spike (`openspec/changes/audio/spike-results.md`) pitted three routes for servicing APU register accesses on macOS arm64: (a) fault trapping with an A64 decoder, (b) lifter-emitted MMIO accessors, (c) polling/shadow pages. (a) won: 2.16 us per trapped access against 52 us under Wine, about 460 accesses/s in steady state, no lifter change, and pointer-derived DSOUND accesses (`edi = 0xFE804028` loops) that (b) cannot see statically.

## Decisions

- One decoder per architecture, both header-only: `mmio_decode.h` (x86-64, VEH) and `mmio_decode_a64.h` (AArch64, signals). The A64 one refuses what it does not understand (exclusive, acquire/release, atomics) and returns 0, which the hook logs as `[APU] MMIO decode fail` with the opcode.
- The POSIX device backend keeps the `xa2_*` names and contract so `mcpx_apu_pace_step` and the tests are shared; `XA2_BACKEND_NAME` names the backend in the log. SDL's push queue has no slot limit, so `RECOMP_AUDIO_BUF_COUNT` is a pacing mark there, not a capacity.
- Headless means silent: `RECOMP_HEADLESS=1` picks SDL's `dummy` audio driver (which drains the queue in real time) unless `SDL_AUDIODRIVER` names another; to hear a headless run, set `SDL_AUDIODRIVER=coreaudio`.
- Golden reference PNGs are not copied into this worktree; `analysis/golden/frames` is a symlink to the main checkout's directory (gitignored either way).
- `scripts/audio_check.py` fails a dump that is noise-like, not only one that is silent, clipped or torn: a 1 s window at or above the silence floor is noise-like when its spectral flatness (200 Hz-16 kHz) is at or above `noise_flatness` (0.30) and at least `noise_hf_share` (25 %) of its energy is above 8 kHz; more than `max_noise_fraction` (10 %) such windows fail the scenario. White noise measures 0.56 flatness and 67 % up there, this title's music under 0.3 and under 16 %. Two conditions, because a dense mix can reach 0.3 flatness without being noise, and nothing musical here puts a quarter of its energy above 8 kHz. The dumps that played memory as PCM had 0.4-0.5 and 57-67 %; every check in the script had passed them.
- A run of digital zero is a dropout only with sound at or above the silence floor in the 50 ms on each side: the opening movie's dithered silence touches zero and is not a dropout; the earlier counts (5, 23, 6) included those.

## Findings: the speaker test (2026-10-03)

The build at cat c989518 passed every offline check and the user heard static, then faint music under pulsating noise -- on the Mac, and on the Proton replay too. Three defects in the shared APU model, none of them in the macOS code:

1. `mcpx_apu_phys` folded every guest physical address at or above 64 MB into the low 64 MB (`a & 0x03FFFFFF`). cat maps 128 MB (`XBOX_DEVKIT_RAM`), and BLiNX 2's heap, where DirectSound's buffers live (`0x038C0000..0x08000000`), is mostly above 64 MB: every effect and ADPCM voice read `.text` or whatever sat 64 MB below its buffer (voice 68: rms 17156, zero-crossing rate 0.57, full-scale white noise), the 64 KB music ring (voice 70) crossed the boundary once a lap (the 16384-sample pulsation), and voice 69, whose ring sat below 64 MB, was the faint music, about 10 dB under the noise. Addresses below `g_xbox_total_ram` now resolve to RAM; the fold stays for addresses above it. `[APU-VSTAT]` prints each voice's class (`ram`, `win`, `img`, `hiwin`, `wrap`); a 60 s run has 0 `wrap`.
2. `voice_resample` ignored its `rate`: 44.1 kHz material (rate 1.088) played a semitone and a half high and 8.8 % fast, and stream voices drained faster than the title filled them. It is a linear-interpolation resampler now (`rs_last/rs_next/rs_pos` per voice); the `[APU-VSTAT]` source-frame count for the music voices is 44098/s at 48000 output frames/s (ratio 1.0885, expected 1.0884).
3. The frame thread, with the APU on but the front end trapped (a voice went idle under `FETFORCE1`, waiting for DirectSound's service routine to clear `FECTL`), took the lightweight branch: it advanced the frame counter and handed the device a period with that frame's 32 samples silent. One hole per trapped frame, four or five in a row when the routine ran a DPC later: the 32-160 sample zero runs in the music every few seconds, on the Mac and under Proton (whose longer holes were IRQ hold-offs of the same kind). xemu produces no frame while trapped and re-checks; so does the model now (`fe-trapped-frames` in the `[APU-IRQ]` line; 30 in 60 s, 0 holes).

Also folded in from the review: `apu_mmio_rd/wr` split an 8-byte access into two 4-byte model accesses (the model ignores `size`); `xbox_Ac97PosixFault` is serialised with a spinlock (the RW window of one thread's fault no longer lets another thread's write through unemulated; the residual race, two faults on the same page at once, is documented); `s_ac97_stepping` is Windows-only; cat `main.c` requires the fault address to lie in the 4 GB guest window before comparing it with the APU range (a host address far above it used to alias in after truncation). The `FETINTSTS` re-latch in `update_irq` is what xemu does (it is in the original import 7a71ed2) and is not involved: deliveries per trap are what the title's acknowledgement pattern makes them.

These fixes are in the shared model, so they land for Proton with the merge; the Linux/Proton host was down during this change, so that verification is open (task 5.3).

## Measurements (macOS arm64, M4 Max, headless, 2026-10-03)

- 60 s `@attract` with the dummy device: first sound 2.96 s, 5 dropouts (two of 130 ms in the opening movie's first second, the 11.4 s of `title_movie_1a`, whose `song_TITLE.adx` is missing on Proton too, and two single-period holes at 45 s and 51 s), clipping 0, 0 `KeBugCheck`, 0 device underruns, se_frames about 1500/s. Proton baseline (run 20261003-113726, 75 s): 23 dropouts, clipping 0, 0 KeBugCheck.
- 105 s `@stage1`: script done at 36.2 s, no hang, 6 dropouts (five before the stage is loaded, one of 4 ms), clipping 0, 0 KeBugCheck.
- Golden `@attract` with the CPU rasteriser: audio on against audio off differs by mae <= 3.0 and bad <= 0.045 % on flips 299-303 (frames unchanged); the Mac CPU path fails the Proton reference either way and runs at about 282 ms per flip, so the second golden frame is out of reach in 75 s.
- Before the SDL2 backend and the clock fix the same `@attract` run had 42-47 % digital silence in regular 130 ms holes.

## Measurements after the speaker-test fixes (same host, headless, dummy device)

- Noise, `audio_check.py` `--scenario boot-title`, 60 s `@attract`: before (cat c989518, the build the user heard) 5 of 45 windows noise-like, flatness median 0.32 max 0.51, energy above 8 kHz up to 67 %, 5 dropouts; Proton 4.1 baseline (run 20261003-113726) 5 of 54 noise-like, median 0.28 max 0.42, up to 57 % above 8 kHz, 23 dropouts; after: 0 of 44 noise-like, median 0.18 max 0.30, at most 16 % above 8 kHz, 0 dropouts, clipping 0, 0 device underruns, PASS. Per voice: the effects voices went from rms 12000-17000 / zero-crossing rate 0.25-0.57 to rms 2000-8000 / 0.03-0.25; the music voice 69 is unchanged apart from the pitch.
- Pitch: voice 69's source consumption 44098 frames/s (ratio 1.0885 to the 48 kHz output; expected 1.0884); with the first resampler draft (one source sample dropped per frame) it was 45477/s, which an alignment against the voice's own 44.1 kHz dump put at 1.0554.
- 100 s `@stage1`: no hang, 0 `KeBugCheck`, 0 of 89 windows noise-like (median 0.17, max 0.26, at most 12 % above 8 kHz); the trapped-frame fix is measured in the run recorded in tasks 6.5.
