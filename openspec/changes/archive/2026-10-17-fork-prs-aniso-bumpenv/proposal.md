## Why

On upstream sp00nznet/xboxrecomp#184, hgeorgilas (the `spartan` fork, Spartan: Total Warrior) agreed to contribute pieces of his D3D11 work onto our backend seam, one PR per feature, D3D11 side only, with the Metal twins on us. The first two are open against our public fork's `blinx2/portability`:

- **apexJCL/xboxrecomp#1, `render.aniso`** (e5a857a, `pr/hgeorgilas-aniso`): anisotropic filtering, 2..16, through a new enhancement key `RECOMP_RENDER_ANISO`, applied only to a texture that has mip levels, whose minification filters, and that is not a cube map. Render targets read back, movie frames and lookup tables have no mips and keep the stock filter (in Spartan they shimmer under it). Carried in `nv2a_host_opts.aniso`, part of the D3D11 sampler key. 39 lines over six files, already through the `recomp_env` X-macro table.
- **apexJCL/xboxrecomp#2, BUMPENVMAP and BUMPENVMAP_LUMINANCE** (a061141, `pr/hgeorgilas-bumpenv`): texture-shader modes 6 and 7 in the shared combiner generator. The executor keeps `SET_SHADER_OTHER_STAGE_INPUT` (0x1E78); `nv2a_stage_decode` reads the stage's `BUMP_ENV_MAT`, `SCALE` and `OFFSET`; `nv2a_ps_consts` gains `bump[4]` and `bumpls[4]` (Metal's layout asserts move to 544/560); the HLSL generator samples a mode-6 stage at `(s, t) + M * (du, dv)`, with `du, dv` the input stage's blue and green as signed bytes, and mode 7 scales by the input's red times `SCALE` plus `OFFSET`. Only the D3D11 backend sets `bump_on`; Metal gets the struct and still samples these modes as plain 2D. His open question: the four matrix registers hold 00, 01, 11, 10, inferred from Spartan's colour grade, and he asks for a second title.

Both are based on public ac27175 (private 10d1611) and apply to `posix-host/portability` (6c06ae4) with no conflict (`git merge-tree`: 0).

**They matter to our titles, not only to Spartan.** BUMPENVMAP has been a TASKS follow-up since the metal-boss work and again from the tracker item: BLiNX 2's Boss 3 ripple and the stage-1 ocean use it, and the end-of-run `[VSH]` reports show mode 6 on stages 1 and 2 in 520 attract batches and 3540 stage-1 batches (`runs/nits/base-cpu-attract`, `nits-cpu-stage1`), and on stage 1 in 3808 Burnout 3 race batches (`runs/b3audio/runs/20261004-200146-H-race-A`). No run of any title shows mode 7. Spartan's glow pass reads the R8B8 table through mode 6, and #184's bump-env comparison depends on it.

## What Changes

- **Both PRs come in locally, each as its own commit with hgeorgilas as author** (`Harry <harrygeorgilas@gmail.com>`, `git cherry-pick -x` of the PR heads onto a toolkit branch off `posix-host/portability`). The public branch moves only through the round script, so nothing is merged on GitHub; each PR is closed with a link to its public commit once the round lands. Review fixes to his code go in our commits, named in the close comment, never folded into his.
- **Metal twins, as our commits:** `maxAnisotropy` in the Metal sampler on the same rule and in the same key bit; the MSL generator's mode 6 and 7 branch, the Metal backend's `tex_input` and `bump_on`, and a shared input-stage helper both backends and the test call.
- **The matrix order is settled by reference and confirmed by frame as far as the titles allow** (design D1): the XDK's texture-stage enum (via Cxbx-Reloaded's mirror) and xemu's method handler both say the registers hold 00, 01, 11, 10. The PR's code is right; its comments say xemu reads 00, 01, 10, 11, which is wrong, and our commit corrects them. Spartan's diagonal matrix confirms only that slot 2 is m11; whether slots 1 and 3 are m01, m10 or the transpose needs a title that writes a non-diagonal matrix. A one-time log of every matrix BLiNX 2 and Burnout 3 write finds one or says there is none, in which case the transpose stays open in TASKS. The order is one hardware fact and lives in the shared backend code, never in a per-game quirk.
- **A standalone ctest** for the stage decode, the constants and the generated text, a bump case in both split tests (the MSL one compiles on the Mac), and a `bump` trace key that logs each distinct bump configuration once with its flip.
- **Goldens:** attract and stage1 frames that show water are expected to move. The change treats them as improvements to be judged against the xemu captures and re-recorded after the user's playtest (design D5), never as regressions to be tuned away.
- **CPU rasteriser:** modes 6 and 7 keep sampling as 2D; it builds, and the gap goes in TASKS.
- **Out of scope:** MSAA and FXAA (hgeorgilas sends them after running them in a game), the `SIGNED` filter bits, the in-game menu entry for `render.aniso` (read at start, as his PR says), the HLE D3D8 layer.

## Impact

- **Backends:** D3D11 and Metal. Behaviour changes at stock for every title that uses mode 6: BLiNX 2 (attract, stage 1, Boss 3), Burnout 3 (race) and Spartan (glow and colour grade). `render.aniso` is opt-in and defaults off.
- **Hosts:** Mac (Metal), the Deck under Proton standing in for the Linux/Proton host (D3D11, Mac-built exe), native Windows only if the user chooses it for the playtest.
- **Goldens:** attract-cliff and the stage1 frames may change verdict on both backends; story should not (no water). Judged and re-recorded per design D5; Metal and D3D11 must agree on the re-recorded references. The golden harness gains `render.aniso` in its non-stock check (a CLI change that rides the next CLI round).
- **Toolkit:** yes. Bump-env is a candidate for the upstream GPU series (G3 combiner decoder, G6 and G7 backends), with his authorship kept when the series is cut; `render.aniso` stays fork-only in the enhancements layer unless upstream asks for plain opt-in keys (`notes/upstream-spartan/REPORT.md`, item 6). The `d3d8_xbox.h` texture-stage enum carries the PC order (10 before 11), which joins the tracker item's follow-up on that header.
- **cat:** two `docs/env.md` rows (`RECOMP_RENDER_ANISO`, trace `bump`), TASKS, possibly re-recorded golden references; the toolkit pin moves at the next round as usual.
- **Public:** the round script rewrites every author to the noreply identity today; it needs to keep a foreign author, or his two commits reach the public branch under our name (tasks 6.1).
- **Needs the user:** the playtest of BLiNX 2 and Burnout 3 before the merge, the host for any golden re-record (the references were taken on the Linux/Proton host's RTX 2060; the Deck stands in), the text of the PR close comments, and the round-script change.
