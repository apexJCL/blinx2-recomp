## Why

On BLiNX 2's Story Mode / LOAD GAME screen (title state 0xD: the 3D cat in the yellow suit and the camo character on the left) the characters vanish at random intervals on the user's native Windows PC (investigation by agent blx-loadcat, evidence in `runs/loadcat/`, diagnostic branch `diag/loadcat` aaee8d6 in `wt/loadcat/xboxrecomp`, not for merge). The title draws them only when its own visibility tests say they are visible: two tests a flip (`BeginVisibilityTest` / `EndVisibilityTest` / `GetVisibilityTestResult`, which are NV097 `CLEAR_REPORT_VALUE`, `SET_ZPASS_PIXEL_COUNT_ENABLE` and `GET_REPORT`), issued by `sub_00046CE0` as a screen-space quad around a projected point and read by the player code `sub_00022DB0` from the table at 0x9FB930, where `count < threshold` skips the draw (pb-index-batch-cap design, "Occlusion, ruled out"). A test the title cannot read counts as hidden.

Since toolkit 377f04a and 65b25c5 (2026-10-02) a `GET_REPORT` on a GPU backend is answered asynchronously. `nv2a_occ` (`src/kernel/nv2a_backend_common.c`) turns each counting stretch into a host occlusion query, puts the report on a pending list, and leaves the report's status word at 0xFFFFFFFF, which `GetVisibilityTestResult` reads as `D3DERR_TESTINCOMPLETE`, until a poll finds every query's result: at every batch and at the flip without a flush, on the ack thread's idle tick with one. A report still pending after 250 ms, or with more than eight stretches, completes as visible (0x10000). A title that reads the report before the result is in sees TESTINCOMPLETE and treats the characters as hidden: 157 batches on that flip instead of 1509, the characters gone for the frame. The mechanism is in main 6c06ae4 and 88874d6 and on every in-flight branch; it is not a fork-prs regression.

Evidence:

- Mac, Metal, LOAD GAME idle: 7864 flips at 1509 batches and none at 157 (`runs/loadcat/mac-lgidle-152138`); every one of the 3994 flips with two reports shows `pending 0` at the flip. Deck, D3D11 under Proton, scale 1 and 4: 0 missing; `pending 0` at all but two or three flips a run (3, 11 and 23 pending at the loading transitions).
- Forced invisible (the diag's `LOADCAT_OCC=0`): both characters gone, 157 batches on every flip.
- Injected result latency (the diag's `LOADCAT_OCC_DELAY_US`): 8 ms on the Mac, the cat missing in 190 of 301 flips; 16 ms, 295 of 301 (3644 flips at 157 batches and none at 1509, `pending 2` at 461 flips; `mac-lgdelay16000-153708`); 16 ms on the Deck, 300 of 300.
- Natural result age, from `GET_REPORT` to the poll that found the result (the diag's `[OCCAGE]` histogram, `mac-lgage-153906`): per 600 reports about 410 under 1 ms, 40 under 2, 145 under 4, 5 under 8 and none above on the Mac; about 8% at 8-16 ms on the Deck, with no vanishing.
- Native Windows: not measured yet. The four logs in `runs/loadcat/winlogs/` were taken without `RECOMP_TRACE=flip`, so they carry neither the per-flip batch counts nor the pending counts. The hypothesis is irregular host-GPU latency at `render.scale` 4 (2560x1920, 1509 batches), where the queries of frame N finish after the title's read in frame N+1.

What the console guaranteed: the NV2A writes the report from the pipeline when `GET_REPORT` comes through, after the fragments counted before it, and the CPU never waits for it; D3D's swap throttle keeps the CPU within about a frame of the GPU, so a test issued in frame N is written by the time frame N+1 reads it, and the title's "skip when incomplete" path never ran on this screen. The injected delays place the title's read 4-12 ms after its own `GET_REPORT`, at the start of the next frame, after the swap. Our port keeps that invariant only while the host GPU returns the query within the frame; when it runs a frame behind, the title reads an answer the console never gave.

## What Changes

- **A report is settled at the flip** (design D1, D2): a shared `nv2a_occ_flip()` in `nv2a_backend_common.c`, called by both GPU backends inside their flip once the frame is submitted (D3D11 after `Present`, Metal after the flip's `flush(1)` wait). It polls with a flush, waits at most `occ_settle_us` (default 0), and settles every report still pending: count then status 0, the count being the last completed count for that report address, or `NV2A_OCC_VISIBLE` when the address has none yet. The settled report keeps its queries on a retired list; when their result arrives it updates only the address's last count, never the report, so a title's reset of the slot is never overwritten and a steadily lagging GPU gives counts exactly one frame stale.
- **Nothing else in the tracker moves**: `GET_REPORT`, the stretches, the supersede rule, the overflow and 250 ms fallbacks, `sync` and `fixed` modes, the CPU path's synchronous counts and the render-scale division are as they are.
- **Two debug keys** (design D3): `occ_delay_us` holds every result back (the test hook the harness case and the Deck/Mac reproductions use; it replaces the diag's `LOADCAT_OCC_DELAY_US`) and `occ_settle_us` bounds the wait at the flip. Both in `recomp_env.h`'s debug tier with rows in `docs/env.md`. The flip log's `occ` fields gain `settled N (stale M, first K)` and the end-of-run summary the totals.
- **Tests** (design D4): the standalone ctest `tests/occ_reports` drives `nv2a_occ` with fake query ops and a fake clock; the d3d11 smoke's cross-frame reuse case is rewritten, since it relied on a report surviving a flip pending.
- **A harness case** (design D5): the preset `@story-idle` (the pad script `runs/loadcat/bin/loadgame-idle.txt`), the log checker `scripts/occ_flips.py` with its pytest, and the case itself: LOAD GAME idle with `occ_delay_us=16000` gives 1509 batches on every flip once the screen is up, on Metal and on D3D11, where the base gives 157.
- **Out of scope:** what the count means against the NV2A (TASKS: the open fidelity item); readers that poll the result inside the frame that issued it (the attract cliff's flare and glow sprites, which Metal's commit at `GET_REPORT` already serves); the title's own fallback.

## Impact

- **Backends:** both through the shared tracker; the backend parts are one call each in the flip and the flip-log fields (design D2). At stock on Metal the settle never fires: the flip's `flush(1)` waits for every command buffer, so no result is outstanding after it. At stock on D3D11 under DXVK a report is pending at the flip on one to three flips a scenario (the startup burst of 23 and the loading transitions); those now settle at the flip instead of completing during the next frame. The goldens on both must pass unchanged (design D6).
- **Hosts:** Mac (Metal, headless), the Deck under Proton standing in for the Linux/Proton host (D3D11, Mac-built exe), and native Windows on the user's PC, the host where it was seen; that run needs the user's go and uses drive D (memory notes *deck-proton-bench*, *windows-pc-drive-d*).
- **Goldens:** attract, stage1 and story unchanged on Metal and D3D11, no re-record. Burnout 3 uses visibility tests too (15-20 reports a flip in a race, 4 on some screens, 0 in the menus: the flip logs in `runs/b3jitter/` and `runs/present-scanout/`), so the race gate runs on Metal and on the Deck.
- **Toolkit:** yes: `src/kernel/nv2a_backend_common.{c,h}`, `src/d3d/nv2a_pb_d3d11.c`, `src/d3d/nv2a_pb_metal.m`, `src/platform/recomp_env.h`, the hook comment in `src/kernel/nv2a_pb_state.h`, `tests/occ_reports/`, `tests/d3d11_backend_smoke/smoke.c`. Branch `fix/occ-reports` off `posix-host/portability`. Title-neutral, an upstream candidate with the visibility series (377f04a, 65b25c5).
- **cat:** `docs/env.md` (two debug rows, the Debug count 91 to 93), `src/pad_input.c` and `game.toml` (the preset), `README.md`'s preset row, `scripts/occ_flips.py` and `scripts/test_occ_flips.py`, TASKS. `b3`: nothing in the repo.
- **Overlap:** `feat/fork-prs-aniso-bumpenv`, `fix/present-scanout` and `fix/present-mode-height` touch the same files in other functions; one adjacent hunk in `nv2a_pb_d3d11.c`'s flip-log statics (design D7).
- **Needs the user:** the go for the native Windows run, before and after, on the PC they play on. The default of `occ_settle_us` (0) is the recommendation; that run is where a different value would show its worth.
