# The tracker item recording script: @story-hub with slack at the steps that wait on
# the wall clock, so that a flip-timed record of it replays. R0_opening is
# not skipped: its skip opens 3-9 s in (3.2 s in the recording run), by the
# movie decoder's clock, so a press at a fixed flip may land before it.
# tsedit gets 10 s to settle, and two spare A taps follow the save (the hub
# load ignores them).
wait open logo_mgs.sfd
tap START until open blinx2_opening.sfd
tap START until open title_movie
wait mem 0x5eb5f4 == 0x5eb620
wait mem 0x5eb620 == 0
tap START every 1 until open R0_opening.sfd
wait open tsedit
wait 10
A/30p
wait polls 476
tap A every 90p until mem 0x5eb620 == 0xd
wait polls 416
tap A every 90p until open blinx2data.bin
wait polls 90
A/45p
wait polls 90
A/45p
wait open song_HUBsw
