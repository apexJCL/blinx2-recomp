## Context

`sub_00042E50` (0x42E50-0x4301F) reads `[0x4B271C]` +0x0C, +0x10, +0x14 (the angles) and +0x2C..+0x34 (the eye). It reads the projection block `[0xADA030]`, which `stage_render` sets to 0xAAA8E0 just before its own call. It builds rotations, a pi turn and a 0.5 scale and offset, and writes `c[0x74..0x77]` through `sub_002E45D0`. That maps a vertex to its place in a screen-sized texture. `sub_00042AE0` (`cam_projection_setup`) calls it inside every build, where the struct holds the free fields. It has 30 call sites in all. One is that in-build call; the rest run outside a build (`stage_render`, the HUD and stage passes, the title menus), and some are duplicate bodies at overlapping entries.

## Decisions

- **D1. Swap around `sub_00042E50`, the way the builds are swapped.** It goes through `cam_build` with `who = NULL`. The swap and its restore are the same, but the call does not add to `s_cam_calls`, so `cam_log=n` and `cam_fix=hold:N` count builds as before. It writes no camera field, so the restore puts back every field.
  - Rejected: re-running it after each build. That would not help, because the passes call it again later.
  - Rejected: keeping the free fields in the struct for the whole render. The game's camera logic and its other readers would then see the free camera, which is against D1.
- **D2. The other readers of the struct stay as they are.**
  - `sub_0003B750` reads the eye for the cull centres. Its update-phase callers (`sub_001C2610`, `sub_001DF1E0`, `ent_view_level_reset`) feed the in-view counters at +0x2B4. Swapping it would change gameplay, not just pop-in. The free-camera design accepts this in its Risks section ("Other culls").
  - Render-path readers of the eye or angles that run outside any build were left; D3 settles them. The LOD and audio readers keep the game's camera, as the free-camera design's Risks section says.
- **D3. The render-path readers.** Each one was read in the generated code and its callers traced; the stage 1 flights are in `runs/ITEM/`. The readers that draw relative to the camera get the D1 wrapper (`cam_build(..., NULL, ...)` on the whole function). A function wrap is enough for each one: the camera reads sit in their own bodies, and the builds and readers they call nest under the outer swap.
  - `gfx_stage_pass_e58d0` (`sub_000E58D0`; with the wrapper, gen calls the body `sub_000E58D0_gen`): **swapped.** `sub_00068E70` calls it at 0x69055, between builds. When `[0xAF0EE0]` is not 1 and `[0xB87168]` is 0, 3, 5, 0xF, 0x11, 0x12 or 0x28, it translates the sky dome to (eye.x, -3000, eye.z), scaled by `cam_far`, at 0xE5A06 after its `sub_00216D70` build. So that dome is centred on the eye, and without the swap it stayed around the game camera. `[0xAF0EE0]` is the number of views: `sub_000606F0` sets it to 1 at 0x6190D for one player and to the player count at 0x618B4 for split screen, building one `cam_views` entry per player. So the eye translate runs only in split screen. Flights on stage_current 0, 5, 0xA, 0xF and 0x14 all logged `[0xAF0EE0] = 1` and never reached 0xE5A06 (`runs/ITEM/sky-*`, a probe on the `sub_002D4CB0` call that returns to 0xE5A27). The path is untested (task 3.6). Its callees also draw from the camera: `sub_000E61A0` (billboards through `sub_000DAE80` and `sub_000DAEC0`) and `sub_000E6FA0` (`sub_001D72E0`).
  - `sub_001F73C0`: **swapped.** `sub_00068E70` calls it at 0x69B24, after the builds. It builds quads that face the eye through `sub_001F7FB0(eye, pos)`.
  - `sub_001D72E0`: **no wrapper of its own.** It translates to the eye and draws models through `sub_00038270`, so it should follow the camera. Its only caller is `sub_000E6FA0`, which is called from the stage pass (0xE5E98) and from `sub_00219940` inside the `sub_0021B1F0` build. Both are now under a swap.
  - `sub_000DAEC0`: **swapped.** It turns a sprite by the camera angles +0x0C..+0x1C so that it faces the view. Of its two callers, `sub_000DAE80` runs inside the stage pass and `sub_000DAE50` is only reached through a pointer, so the wrapper sits on the callee both share; nested, it is a plain call. `sub_000DAE80` runs only when `[0xB87168]` is 0x1C. It loops over the player table 0xB22BA4 and calls `sub_000DAEC0` (eax = the entry) for entries whose first byte is 0x6B. At 0xDB13A, `sub_000DAEC0` also stores the camera pitch (+0x0C, kept at esp+0x34 on entry) into that entry's +0x44. With the free camera, that field takes the free pitch on stage 0x1C. +0x44 is written by `ent_player_update` and many others and read by `sub_000C0670`, `C2E20`, `C5CF0`, `D0B50`, `D9350`, `DB3C0`, `104C20`, `126B20`, `13C670` and `13F740`. No read of the value stored here was confirmed, and stage 0x1C was not flown. This is an open risk, not a verified safe case.
  - `sub_0004D740`: **swapped.** This is the same kind of camera-facing billboard matrix (angles +0x0C..+0x1C), used by 14 call sites in the enemy and effect draws at 0x21DC70..0x27F220 (17 counting the duplicate bodies at overlapping entries).
  - `sub_001A6E00`, the radar (`stage_render`, 0x6860E): **swapped.** It turns the dots by -yaw, read from the struct. The free-camera design records that the user wants the radar to follow the free camera (2026-10-18). Its view cone already followed the free camera before this change. Only the dots stayed on the game's yaw, so the archived free-camera design (design.md:76) was right about the cone only. With the swap, the dots turn with the free camera too (`runs/ITEM/radar-ab.png`, `final-radar-ab.png`).
  - `sub_0015BC40`, the effect draw pass: **swapped.** `sub_00068E70` calls it at 0x69BDE, after the builds. It is also called from `sub_000693E9` (a duplicate body) and from the mode handlers `sub_001C2610` and `sub_001E0DF0` under `sub_00062FB0`. It copies the eye and the pitch, yaw and roll into 0xF2A6FC..0xF2A71C, with the pitch and yaw negated at 0xF2A700/704. Then `sub_0015CE30` and `sub_0015C4A0` turn the particles to face the view from those copies. The only other writer of the copies is the effect step `sub_0015BAA0`, under the stage pass, which now writes them from the free camera. `sub_0015C4A0` is their only reader. Without this wrap, one frame mixed the two cameras. `rand_002D4FF2` cannot be reached from it.
  - The effect step under the stage pass: **kept under the stage-pass swap.** `sub_000E61A0` spawns effects through `sub_0015CE70` (0xE65A0) and steps them through `sub_0015BAA0` (0xE65B6). Those paths read the camera in `sub_0015B950`, `15BEC0`, `15C0D0` and `15DB90`. For camera-attached emitters (`[0xB8CBB8]` cases 1-3), they write the spawn matrix's position into the effect record at +0x8C..+0x94 and +0x98..+0xA0. So while the free camera flies, those emitters spawn at the free camera, as they do at the game camera. The question was whether the number of `rand_002D4FF2` draws can depend on the camera, since that would change the game's random sequence. It cannot:
    - `sub_000E61A0` draws twice at 0xE65E3/0xE65FC. The draws are gated by the countdowns `[0xB87160]` and `[0xB871C4]` and the stage jump table.
    - `sub_0015D310` sets the spawn count from the emitter's age +0x14, window +0x50/+0x5C and rates +0x60/+0x64, through `sub_0015D0A0` (0x15D385). It then loops `sub_0015C0D0` (0x15D3C8).
    - `sub_0015C0D0` takes a record from the free list `[0xB8CBB4]` and always calls `sub_0015D3F0` (0x15C260, 21 draws). `sub_0015D3F0` gates its draws on the record and emitter parameters. The camera is read only at 0x15C18B (case 1), and only to build the spawn matrix.
    - `sub_0015DFD0` calls `sub_0015BD10` (0x15E079, one draw). That call is gated by the record's age +0x18/+0x1C, the emitter's +0x58 and the rates +0xD4..+0xE0. It reads +0x8C..+0x94 only after that, as position terms.
    - None of `sub_0015D0A0`, `15D310`, `15D3F0`, `15DFD0` or `15BD10` reads `[0x4B271C]`. No branch before a draw reads the camera-derived fields.
    - Runtime check on stage 1: a probe read the seed (`sub_002D8149` block +0x14) before and after `sub_000E58D0`. The pass made no draw in any frame: 1919 frames (1132 flying) with the swap, and 1933 and 1932 frames (1234 flying) with the swap skipped (`runs/ITEM/rng-swap`, `rng-skip`, `rng-skip2`). Stage 1 never takes the drawing paths inside the pass, so this run only shows that the swap adds no draws there. The static reading above carries the verdict. The swap was not narrowed.
  - Not in scope, as before: `sub_0003B750` and the update-phase culls (D2).
- **D4. The stage 1 sky (`runs/ITEM/fly-far/strip.png`) is fixed in the world, as the game draws it, so no swap keeps it around the free camera.**
  - In stage 1, as in every single-player stage, `[0xAF0EE0]` (the view count) is 1, so the stage pass skips the 0xE5A06 eye translate (D3). It draws the sky through its stage model loop instead (`sub_000E4310`, model 0x01A091DC through `sub_00038840`), with an identity matrix and a pi turn. The dome is a world-fixed model around the level, and the game camera never leaves it.
  - The flight in the strip sinks and moves forward. The `cam_log` run moves the free eye from (360, -35, -1302) to (360, 380, 243): about 415 units down (Y is down) and 1545 forward. Below the floor nothing is drawn, so the lower screen is the clear colour (black).
  - As the eye sinks under the dome's rim, the visible sky shrinks to a band at the top of the screen. Once the eye has passed out of the dome, nothing is drawn ahead.
  - Turning back on the same flight shows the dome again from far away (`runs/ITEM/base-flyfar/strip.png`, last frame). The model is still drawn; it is just behind the camera.
  - With every D3 swap in place, the far-flight frames match the unswapped build apart from the radar (`runs/ITEM/fix-flyfar`, `base-flyfar`).
  - This is the game's own behaviour: the dome is a model in the world, like the level, and a camera that flies out of the level leaves it behind. It stays as it is, with no follow-up (the user, 2026-11-05). The far-flight gate checks the draws that follow the camera (the radar and the billboards), not the stage 1 sky.
## Risks

- A caller that needs the game's own screen projection while flying. None was found: every call site found feeds a draw.
